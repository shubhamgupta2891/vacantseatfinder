Privacy Policy

App: Vacant Seat Finder

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Introduction

Vacant Seat Finder helps users view railway coach composition and vacant berth information after chart preparation. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

The app does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to use its core features.

When you search for a train, the app may process the train number, journey details, station selections, and related search parameters needed to retrieve railway information.

Network Requests and Third-Party Services

The app connects to railway-related online services to request train schedules, coach composition, chart information, and vacant berth availability. These services may receive the information included in requests and may process technical information such as IP addresses according to their own policies.

The app does not claim ownership of or affiliation with Indian Railways or IRCTC unless explicitly stated by the relevant authorities.

Data Storage and Sharing

The app does not intentionally sell users' personal information. Search information may be processed or stored locally as needed for app functionality. Information transmitted to third-party services is subject to those services' privacy practices.

Permissions and Security

The app uses network access to retrieve railway information. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. It does not require account registration to use its core features.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact the app developer at the support email address provided on the app's Google Play Store listing.
